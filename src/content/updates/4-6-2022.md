## Custom Email Domains

Professional plan users can now connect their own email domain so that clients receive branded email notifications. If your website is agency123.com, for example, you can use notifications@agency123.com to send client email notifications. This feature will improve email deliverability to your clients (i.e. fewer emails going to spam) so we recommend that you set it up. You can connect a custom email domain on the Settings &gt; Domains page.
