## 2025 Year in Review

Companies will say every year is the “big year.” But 2025 was really, actually a big year for Assembly, and for customers like you who trust us to elevate your clients' experience.

Here’s a snapshot of how you all are using Assembly in 2025—and what mattered most to you.

- More than 20,000 tasks were created in the Tasks App, with the steepest growth in the second half of the year. Half went to your clients (50%), a third to your teammates (34%), and the rest to your clients' companies (15%).
- More than 45,000 automations ran. The most popular one for professional firms was also the simplest: when a new client logs in, send them a contract to sign.
- November was your busiest month of the year, with May close behind. July was the quietest.
- The top three reasons you chose Assembly: improving your clients' experience, centralizing client information, and streamlining client onboarding.

Thank you for your faith and trust in us as we've worked to grow and change the platform. We know there are still plenty of areas where we can make things even more flexible, customizable, and useful for you and your clients, but I hope your interactions with me and our team prove that we're as committed as you are to a great customer experience.
