## Improvements and Updates

- Added the ability to delete invoices that have status *void*. Deleted invoices are permanently hidden to internal users and clients.
- Added the ability to delete subscriptions that have status *cancelled*. Deleted subscriptions are permanently hidden to internal users and clients.
- Improved the performance of our authentication flows – Log in, sign up, reset password, etc.
- Improved the design of the *Customizations* page, *Settings &gt; Profile* page, and the *Settings &gt; API* page.
