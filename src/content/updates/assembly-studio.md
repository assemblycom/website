## Build apps in Assembly with AI

Assembly's AI app builder is here. Describe a custom onboarding experience, project tracker, or client dashboard, and Assembly builds it into your client experience, already connected to your contacts, team, permissions, and branding. Unlike other app builders, the AI never touches the security layer: authentication, permissions, and client scoping stay on Assembly's trusted infrastructure. You can try building your own apps by clicking on “Add App” on the sidebar.

![](/images/updates/2026/08/The-AI-app-builder-for-professional-service-firms-1.webp)

Our team also built 20+ new apps for you, each tested, secured, and covering the workflows professional service firms run every day. Click "Browse all apps" to see them all: proposal creators, document collectors, AI assistants, design approvals, client help desks, community hubs, time trackers, resource libraries, events &amp; RSVPs, and more. And because every app is built on the app builder, every app is remixable: open one, switch to build mode, and describe what you'd change.
